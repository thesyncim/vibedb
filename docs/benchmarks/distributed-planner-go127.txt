VibeDB distributed planner/statistics kernel measurements
Date: 2026-09-03
Base: origin/main 09e60689
Toolchain: go1.27.0 darwin/arm64
CPU: Apple M4 Max
Method: five repetitions, 500ms minimum per benchmark, medians reported in ../distributed-optimizer.md.
These are in-process kernel measurements, not distributed throughput or competitor comparisons.
The index-routing benchmark applies the same compiled index 16 times to an 8 KiB document to isolate parse reuse.
The local grouping benchmark uses 10,000 disjoint groups in 4096-row batches.
The sketch benchmark unions two 2048-entry sketches using existing repeated insertion or sequential merge.
